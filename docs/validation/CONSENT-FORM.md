# Speaker consent form: Gyara Hausa speech recordings

*This form is written in plain English to follow the spirit of the Nigeria
Data Protection Act 2023. It is not legal advice. Have it checked by someone
qualified before relying on it.*

**Form number:** `[[GY-001]]`  (copy this into `data/REGISTER.csv`, column `consent_form`)

---

## What this is for

Gyara is a team building free tools to make Hausa speech-to-text more
accurate. We are asking to record your voice, or to use a recording of you,
to **train and test Hausa speech recognition**.

Nobody will use your recording to identify you, to watch you, to judge you,
or to copy your voice.

## What we collect

- The recording of your voice.
- A written transcript of what you said.
- Optional, only if you agree: your dialect or home area, gender, and age
  range (for example 18–25). These help us see where the AI works badly.
  You can leave them blank.

We do **not** need your phone number, address or ID. Your name stays on this
form only. In our data you are a code, like `spk_014`.

## Who sees it

- The Gyara team.
- Judges of the National AI Innovation Challenge 2026 may hear short samples.
- Only if you tick (b) or (c) below: the public.

## Choose how we may use your recording

Tick each one you agree to. You can agree to (a) only.

- [ ] **(a) Internal testing only.** We use it to measure and improve our
  tools. It is never published.
- [ ] **(b) Open dataset.** It may be included in a dataset we publish
  online for anyone to download and use for research and speech technology.
  Once published, copies cannot be fully recalled.
- [ ] **(c) Published model.** It may be used to train a speech model that we
  publish online. Your voice is not in the model file, but the model learned
  from it. A published model cannot be "untrained".

## Your rights

- **Saying no is fine.** Nothing happens to you if you do not sign.
- **You can withdraw at any time.** Contact us (below) with your form number
  or your name. We will delete your recording and transcript from our files
  within 30 days, and leave you out of anything we publish after that. We
  cannot recall copies of a dataset or model already published under (b) or
  (c).
- **You can ask** what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or complain to
  the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## How long we keep it

- (a) only: until the project ends, at most `[[3]]` years, then deleted.
- (b) or (c): for as long as the dataset or model is published.

Recordings are stored on access-controlled team storage, never in a public
code repository.
